	  - (a) a national policy statement:

## Clause 2 — Overview of transition to new system
  - (1) When this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026 receive Royal assent,—
  - (a) activity under those Acts will be focused on making national instruments and preparing planning instruments; and
  - (b) the RMA continues to apply.
  - (2) The transition period will start 1 month after Royal assent and end on the day before the specified transition date. During the transition period,—
  - (a) the RMA continues to apply subject to the amendments made to it in Part 2 of Schedule 12; and
  - (b) national instruments will be issued, regional spatial plans will be notified and decided, and land use plans and natural environment plans will be prepared (in that order).
  - (3) The new legislation will apply—
  - (a) nationally on a specified transition date set by Order in Council if the Minister is satisfied that—
  - (i) all regional spatial plans have been decided; and (ii) all proposed land use plans and natural environment plans have been notified; or
  - (b) in a region or area on a specified transition date set by Order in Council if the Minister is satisfied that it is necessary or desirable to facilitate an orderly transition to the new system as soon as reasonably practicable and that the following has occurred (as applicable):
  - (i) the regional spatial plan for the region has been decided; and (ii) the proposed natural environment plan and each proposed land use plan in the region has been notified; and (iii) the proposed plan for the area (that is not a region or district) has been notified.
  - (4) If the new legislation applies nationally, on and from the specified transition date,—
  - (a) the RMA, including all RMA planning instruments, ceases to apply; and
  - (b) the rest of this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026 apply; and
  - (c) the rules in all notified proposed land use plans and natural environment plans have legal effect; and
  - (d) all land use plans and natural environment plans that have been decided are made operative.
  - (5) If the new legislation applies to a region or an area, on and from the specified transition date,—
  - (a) the consequences described in subclause
  - (4)(a) to
  - (d) apply; but
  - (b) only in relation to the region or area specified in the Order in Council.
  - (6) This clause is only a guide to the general scheme and effect of clauses 3 to 16 of this schedule.
## Clause 3 — Commencement of transition period
  - (1) The transition period commences on the date that is 1 month after this Act receives Royal assent.
  - (2) On the commencement of the transition period,—
  - (a) the amendments to the RMA set out in Part 2 of Schedule 12 come into force; and
  - (b) the RMA, as amended by Part 2 of Schedule 12, continues in force.
## Clause 4 — Order in Council specifying when new legislation applies nationally or to
  - specified regions or areas
  - (1) The Governor General may, by Order in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister,—
  - (a) specify the date on which the new legislation will apply to the whole of New Zealand; or
  - (b) specify the date on which the new legislation will apply to 1 or more specified regions or areas.
  - (2) The Minister may not make a recommendation under subclause
  - (1)(a) unless both the following have occurred:
  - (a) all regional spatial plans have been decided under clause 25 of Schedule 2; and
  - (b) all proposed land use plans and natural environment plans have been notified for submissions under clause 15 of Schedule 3.
  - (3) The Minister may not make a recommendation under subclause
  - (1)(b) unless—
  - (a) the Minister is satisfied that the order is necessary or desirable to facilitate an orderly transition from the RMA to the new legislation as soon as reasonably practicable; and
  - (b) the following (as applicable) have occurred in respect of the region or area to which the order will apply:
  - (i) the regional spatial plan has been decided under clause 25 of Schedule 2; and (ii) the proposed land use plan has been notified for submissions under clause 15 of Schedule 3; and (iii) the proposed natural environment plan has been notified for submissions under clause 15 of Schedule 3; and
  - (c) if a district straddles 2 or more regions, the Minister is satisfied that the order enables all parts of the district to be subject to the new legislation at the same time.
  - (4) The date specified in an order made under subclause
  - (1)(a) or
  - (b) must be a date that is—
  - (a) after 31 December 2027; and
  - (b) no earlier than 6 weeks after the order is made.
  - (5) An Order in Council made under this clause is secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
## Clause 5 — Effect of orders under clause 4
  - (1) If an order made under clause 4(1)(a) specifies the date on which the new legislation applies to the whole of New Zealand, on and from that date,—
  - (a) national rules apply; and
  - (b) the rules in every proposed land use plan and proposed natural environment plan have legal effect despite clause 44 and Part 3 of Schedule 3; and
  - (c) any proposed land use plan, proposed natural environment plan, or rule in a proposed plan that would otherwise have been made or treated as operative in accordance with clause 44 or 60 of Schedule 3 is made or treated as operative; and
  - (d) the provisions described in section 2(3) and
  - (6) of this Act and section 2(2) of the Natural Environment Act 2026 apply; and
  - (e) the RMA, including all RMA instruments, ceases to apply.
  - (2) If an order made under subclause
  - (4)(1)(b) specifies the date on which the new legislation applies to a specified region or area, on and from that date,—
  - (a) national rules apply to the specified region or area; and
  - (b) despite clause 44 and Part 3 of Schedule 3, the following rules have legal effect:
  - (i) rules in a proposed natural environment plan for a specified region and any proposed land use plan in the specified region; and (ii) the rules in a proposed plan for the area (that is not a region or district):
  - (c) any of the following that apply within a specified region or area that would otherwise have been made or treated as operative in accordance with clause 45 or 61 of Schedule 4 is made or treated as operative:
  - (i) a proposed natural environment plan: (ii) a proposed land use plan: (iii) a rule in a proposed plan:
  - (d) the provisions described in section 2(3) of this Act and section 2(2) of the Natural Environment Act 2026 apply to the specified region or area; and
  - (e) the RMA, including all RMA instruments, ceases to apply to the specified region or area.
  - (3) If an order made under clause 4(1)(b) is the final order, the following paragraphs apply instead of subclause
  - (2)(d) and
  - (e):
  - (a) the provisions described in section 2(3) and
  - (6) of this Act and section 2(2) of the Natural Environment Act 2026 apply on the date specified in the order; and
  - (b) the RMA, including all RMA instruments, ceases to apply on that date.
  - (4) Subclauses
  - (1)(e),
  - (2)(e), and
  - (3)(b) do not limit sections 31 to 34 of the Legislation Act 2019.
## Clause 6 — Changes do not apply to region or area that has not transitioned
  - (1) This clause applies if—
  - (a) an order has been made under clause 4(1)(b) in relation to 1 or more specified regions or an area; and
  - (b) the provisions referred to in section 2(3) of this Act and section 2(2) of the Natural Environment Act 2026 have come into force; but
  - (c) the new legislation does not yet apply to another region or area under clause 5(2) (the region or area that has not transitioned).
  - (2) The provisions referred to in section 2(3) of this Act and section 2(2) of the Natural Environment Act 2026 do not apply to the region or area that has not transitioned.
  - (3) The provisions of legislation that are amended, revoked, or repealed by Part 5 of Schedule 12 or section 344 continue to apply to the region or area that has not transitioned as if the legislation had not been amended, revoked, or repealed.
## Clause 7 — Application of requirement to have instrument in place at all times
  - A requirement in this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026 that there must always be an instrument or that an instrument be always or at all times in place is subject to clause 9.
## Clause 8 — References in other legislation to proposed plans that have legal effect
  - (1) This clause applies if any legislation other than this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026 refers to a land use plan or natural environment plan.
  - (2) A proposed land use plan or proposed natural environment plan that has legal effect under clause 5 must be treated as operative for the purposes of that other legislation. First key instruments
## Clause 9 — First key instruments under this Act and Natural Environment Act 2026
  - First set of national instruments under both Acts to be issued
  - (1) No later than 12 months after this Act receives Royal assent, the Minister must take reasonable steps to ensure that—
  - (a) the national policy direction under this Act is issued; and
  - (b) the national policy direction under the Natural Environment Act 2026 is issued.
  - (2) National standards under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026 may not be issued until after national policy direction under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026 (as the case may be) is issued.
  - (3) After the first national policy direction is issued under this Act, the Minister must take reasonable steps to ensure that,—
  - (a) within 12 months after this Act receives Royal assent, national standards providing direction on the structure and form of regional spatial plans are issued; and
  - (b) within 21 months after this Act receives Royal assent, national standards that contain plan provisions to support the development of land use plans are issued.
  - (4) After the first national policy direction is issued under the Natural Environment Act 2026, the Minister must take reasonable steps to ensure that,—
  - (a) within 18 months after that Act receives Royal assent, the following are issued:
  - (i) national standards required by section 53(1)(a) to
  - (d) of that Act (mandatory domains for human health limits): (ii) national standards required by section 66 of that Act (methodologies for setting ecosystem health limits); and
  - (b) within 21 months after that Act receives Royal assent, national standards that contain plan provisions to support the development of natural environment plans are issued. Regional spatial plan to be notified
  - (5) After the first national policy direction is issued under this Act, the spatial plan committee and the local authorities of each region must take reasonable steps to ensure that—
  - (a) the draft regional spatial plan for the region is notified for submissions under clause 18 of Schedule 2 by the later of the following dates:
  - (i) 21 months after Royal assent: (ii) 9 months after national policy direction is issued under this Act and national policy direction is issued under the Natural Environment Act 2026; and
  - (b) the local authorities make and publish their decisions under clause 25 of Schedule 2 within 9 months after the draft regional spatial plan is notified for submissions. Land use plan and natural environment plan to be notified
  - (6) Within 9 months after decisions on a regional spatial plan for a region are made and published under clause 25 of Schedule 2,—
  - (a) each territorial authority in that region must notify a proposed land use plan for public submissions under clause 15 of Schedule 3; and
  - (b) the regional council of the region must notify a proposed natural environment plan, and a proposed land use plan for the coastal marine area, for public submissions under clause 15 of Schedule 3.
  - (7) A territorial authority that is located in 2 or more regions must notify a proposed land use plan for public submissions within 9 months after decisions on the last relevant regional spatial plan are made and published under clause 25 of Schedule 2.
  - (8) If a land use plan or natural environment plan is required for an area that does not require a regional spatial plan, the proposed land use plan or natural environment plan must be notified within 39 months after this Act receives Royal assent.

  - (2) The power conferred by subclause
  - (1)—
  - (a) applies if the proposed national standard is identified as a proposed initial national standard when it is publicly notified under section 65 of this Act or section 92 of the Natural Environment Act 2026; and
  - (b) is subject to subclauses
  - (3) to
  - (5).
  - (3) An initial national standard may identify provisions including national rules and other content as relevant to decision making under the RMA.
  - (4) However, an initial national standard must not—
  - (a) directly amend an RMA plan; or

  - (5) The Minister must be satisfied that the content of an initial national standard will be workable in the context of the RMA, as far as practicable. First regional spatial plans and land use plans
## Clause 11 — Incorporating information from RMA documents into first regional
  - spatial plan
  - (1) The first regional spatial plan and first draft regional spatial plan for a region may incorporate any of the following from an RMA document:
  - (a) information about the state or characteristics of the built environment or natural environment, including information about infrastructure:
  - (b) information that reflects decisions about whether areas or features of the built environment or natural environment have particular characteristics, should be classified in a particular way, or meet criteria of a similar nature.
  - (2) However, information must not be incorporated into a regional spatial plan unless the spatial plan committee and local authorities of the region—
  - (a) are satisfied that the information is consistent with the requirements of this Act that apply to the preparation of regional spatial plans, including the obligation under section 76(3)(a) to implement relevant national instruments under this Act and relevant national instruments under the Natural Environment Act 2026; and
  - (b) have considered whether, in the period since the relevant RMA document was prepared,—
  - (i) any significant new information has become available that supersedes the information in the RMA document: (ii) there has been a significant change in circumstances that is relevant to the content of the regional spatial plan (for example, a major environmental or economic event).
  - (3) If information is incorporated into a draft regional spatial plan,—
  - (a) the spatial plan committee need not comply with section 78 in relation to the information; and
  - (b) the independent hearings panel need not consider or respond to any submission or evidence received on the information when hearing or making recommendations on the draft regional spatial plan, except to the extent the submission or evidence relates to the matters described in subclause
  - (2).
  - (4) The local authorities of the region must reconsider the matters in subclause
  - (2) before making a decision on an independent hearings panel recommendation that relates to content incorporated under this clause.
  - (5) In this clause, RMA document means any of the following documents as defined in section 2(1) of the RMA:
  - (a) a national policy statement:
  - (b) the New Zealand coastal policy statement:
  - (c) a national environmental standard:
  - (d) a regional policy statement:
  - (e) a regional plan:
  - (f) a regional coastal plan:
  - (g) a district plan.

## Clause 12 — Independent hearings panel for first land use plans and natural
  - environment plan
  - (1) This clause applies in relation to the preparation of the first land use plans and natural environment plan in a region.
  - (2) The relevant local authorities must jointly appoint (under clause 6 of Schedule 4) one independent hearings panel to hear—
  - (a) the first proposed land use plan for each district in a region; and
  - (b) the first proposed land use plan for the coastal marine area within the region; and
  - (c) the first proposed natural environment plan for the region.
  - (3) However, if the district of a territorial authority is located in 2 or more regions, the territorial authority must comply with subclause
  - (2)(a) in relation to at least one of those regions.
  - (4) In this clause, relevant local authorities means, in relation to a region,—
  - (a) the regional council; and
  - (b) each territorial authority whose district is in all or part of the region.
## Clause 13 — Proposed plans notified before specified transition date
  - A proposed land use plan or proposed natural environment plan that is notified before the specified transition date—
  - (a) cannot have legal effect before that date despite clause 57 of Schedule 3; and
  - (b) cannot be made operative before that date despite clause 44 or 60 of Schedule 3.
## Clause 14 — References to designations during transition period
  - (1) This clause applies during the transition period for the purpose of preparation for the first regional spatial plans and land use plans.
  - (2) Unless the context otherwise requires,—
  - (a) a reference in this Act to a proposed designation must be read as including a reference to a requirement for a designation under the RMA:
  - (b) a reference in this Act to an existing designation must be read as a reference to an existing designation in a district plan under the RMA.
## Clause 15 — Designations confirmed through first regional spatial plan process before

  - (a) clause 37(3) of Schedule 5 does not apply; and
  - (b) subclauses
  - (2) and
  - (3) apply instead.
  - (2) The territorial authority of the relevant district must, without using a process in Schedule 1 of the RMA,—
  - (a) include the designation or alteration in its district plan and any proposed district plan as if it were a rule in the plan or proposed plan under the RMA; and
  - (b) state in its district plan and any proposed district plan, the name of the requiring authority that has the benefit of the designation or alteration.
  - (3) The designation or alteration commences when it is included in a district plan.

## Clause 17 — Meaning of determined for purposes of clauses 18 to 23
  - In clauses 18 to 23, unless the context otherwise requires, determined,—
  - (a) in relation to an application for a resource consent, means the application has been granted or declined and—
  - (i) the time for lodging appeals in the Environment Court has expired and no appeals have been lodged; or (ii) the Environment Court has determined all appeals or all appellants have withdrawn their appeals; and
  - (b) in relation to the issue of a notice of requirement, means—
  - (i) the time for lodging appeals in the Environment Court has expired and no appeals have been lodged; or (ii) the Environment Court has determined all appeals or all appellants have withdrawn their appeals.
## Clause 18 — Resource consent applications lodged before transition period
  - (1) If an application for a resource consent under the RMA is lodged before the date of commencement of the transition period and is not determined before that date, the application must continue to be processed and determined under the RMA as in force immediately before this Act received Royal assent.
  - (2) See clauses 33 to 37, which provide for the transition of resource consents that have been granted and are current on the specified transition date.
## Clause 19 — Resource consent applications lodged during transition period
  - (1) If an application for a resource consent under the RMA is lodged during the transition period, the application must be processed and determined under the RMA as in force at the time the application is lodged.
  - (2) See clauses 33 to 37, which provide for the transition of resource consents that have been granted and are current on the specified transition date.
  - (3) See also Schedule 12, which contains amendments to the RMA that commence at different times.
## Clause 20 — Notices of requirement issued before transition period
  - If a notice of requirement for a designation or an alteration to a designation under the RMA is issued before the date of commencement of the transition period and is not determined before that date, the notice must continue to be processed and determined under the RMA as in force immediately before this Act received Royal assent.
## Clause 21 — Notices of requirement issued during transition period
  - If a notice of requirement for a designation or an alteration to a designation under the RMA is issued during the transition period and is not determined before the specified transition date, the notice must continue to be processed and determined under the RMA as in force at the time that the notice is issued.
## Clause 22 — Designations confirmed before and after specified transition date
  - (1) If a designation or an alteration to a designation is confirmed and included in the district plan under section 175 of the RMA before the specified transition date, the designation or alteration continues as a designation or alteration under the RMA (see clause 63).
  - (2) If a designation or an alteration to a designation is confirmed under the RMA or in the circumstances described in clause 15 and not included in a district plan before the specified transition date,—
  - (a) the designation or alteration must be included in an operative land use plan and, if applicable, a proposed land use plan under clause 32 of Schedule 5; and
  - (b) if there is no operative land use plan,—
  - (i) the designation or alteration must be treated as if it were included in an operative land use plan under clause 32 of Schedule 5; and (ii) the designation or alteration cannot be altered by decisions on the proposed land use plan; and
  - (c) the designation must be treated as a designation under this Act.
  - (3) If, after the specified transition date, a designation or an alteration to a designation is confirmed under this Act before the first land use plan in the relevant area is made operative,—
  - (a) the designation or alteration must be treated as if it were included in an operative land use plan under clause 32 of Schedule 5; and
  - (b) the designation or alteration cannot be altered by decisions on the proposed land use plan.
## Clause 23 — Pending applications to be requiring authority
  - (1) If an application to become a requiring authority under Part 8 of the RMA is lodged before the specified transition date but not determined by that date, the
  - application must continue to be processed in accordance with section 167 of the RMA as in force at the time the application is lodged.
  - (2) See clause 64. Enforcement processes before and after transition period
## Clause 24 — Enforcement processes under RMA continued
  - (1) To avoid doubt, any enforcement process commenced under the RMA, before the specified transition date must be continued under the RMA.
  - (2) In this clause, enforcement process means any abatement notice, infringement notice, enforcement order, or prosecution under the RMA.
## Clause 25 — Compliance history
  - A consent authority or permit authority may have regard to any previous or current abatement notices, enforcement orders, infringement notices, pecuniary penalties, or convictions under the RMA in accordance with section 170 of this Act or section 170 of the Natural Environment Act 2026.
## Clause 26 — Enforcement officers
  - (1) An enforcement officer authorised under section 38 or 343I of the RMA may continue to perform or exercise all or any of the functions and powers of an enforcement officer under that Act and may perform or exercise all or any of the functions and powers of an enforcement officer authorised under section 275 or 280 of this Act or section 272 or 277 of the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (2) An enforcement officer authorised under section 275 or 280 of this Act or section 272 or 277 of the Natural Environment Act 2026 may perform or exercise all or any of the functions and powers of an enforcement officer authorised under section 38 or 343I of the RMA.
## Clause 27 — Enforcement orders
  - (1) An enforcement order under sections 314 to 321 of the RMA that is in effect immediately before the specified transition date continues to have effect after that date as if it were an enforcement order under sections 259 to 266 of this Act or sections 255 to 262 of the Natural Environment Act 2026 (whichever is applicable).
  - (2) If an application for an enforcement order under the RMA is lodged, but not decided, before the specified transition date, the court must decide the application under the RMA and the order continues to have effect after the specified transition date as if it were an enforcement order under sections 259 to 266 of this Act or sections 255 to 262 of the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (3) If an enforcement order is sought after the specified transition date in respect of conduct to which the RMA applied that occurred before that date, the court may make the order under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (4) Under subclauses
  - (1) to
  - (3), the court must be satisfied that the conduct in respect of which the order is sought is within the scope of an enforcement order that could be made under both the RMA and either this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (5) Sections 259 to 266 of this Act or sections 255 to 262 of the Natural Environment Act 2026 apply to an order made under this clause.
## Clause 28 — Abatement notices
  - (1) An abatement notice issued under section 322 of the RMA that is in effect immediately before the specified transition date continues to have effect after that date as if it were an abatement notice under section 268 of this Act or section 264 of the Natural Environment Act 2026 (whichever is applicable).
  - (2) If an abatement notice is sought after the specified transition date in respect of conduct to which the RMA applied that occurred before that date, the abatement notice may be made under this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (3) The conduct in respect of which the abatement notice is sought must be within the scope of an abatement notice that could be made under both the RMA and either this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026.
## Clause 29 — Water shortage directions
  - (1) A water shortage direction issued under section 329 of the RMA that is in effect immediately before the specified transition date continues to have effect after that date as if it were a water shortage direction under section 271 of the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (2) The direction may be amended or revoked under section 329(3) of the RMA or section 271(3) of the Natural Environment Act 2026.
## Clause 30 — Excessive noise directions
  - (1) An excessive noise direction issued under section 327 of the RMA that is in effect immediately before the specified transition date continues to have effect after that date as if it were an excessive noise direction under section 286 of this Act.
  - (2) The direction is subject to the maximum duration specified in section 327(3) of the RMA and section 286(4) of this Act.
## Clause 31 — Warrant for entry for search
  - A warrant authorising the entry and search of any place or vehicle issued by an issuing officer under section 334 of the RMA that is in effect immediately before the specified transition date continues to have effect after that date as if it were a warrant issued under section 291 of this Act or section 284 of the Nat‐
  - ural Environment Act 2026, subject to any conditions imposed by the issuing officer in the RMA warrant.

  - (1) Proceedings for an offence committed under section 338 of the RMA before the specified transition date may be commenced and completed after that date as if that Act continued to apply, subject to the limitation period in section 338(4) of that Act.
  - (2) An infringement notice issued for an infringement offence under section 343C of the RMA that is in effect immediately before the specified transition date may be enforced after that date as if that Act continued to apply. ## Clause 34 — Arrangements for subdivision consents and reclamation consents
  - (1) If a survey plan has been submitted under section 223 of the RMA for a subdivision but is not approved before the specified transition date, the approval process must continue under the RMA.
  - (2) Once the survey plan for a subdivision is approved under section 223 of the RMA,—
  - (a) the process in Part 2 of Schedule 7 of this Act applies to the further steps; and
  - (b) the 3-year time frame for depositing the survey plan under clause 22(1) of Schedule 7 runs from the time of approval of the survey plan under the RMA.
  - (3) However, if the survey plan for a subdivision has been lodged with the Registrar-General of Land under section 224 of the RMA but is not deposited before the specified transition date, or the survey plan is lodged under section 228 of the RMA but is not approved before the specified transition date, the deposit process continues under the RMA and this Act applies once the deposit process has been completed.

  - (5) Once a plan of survey for a reclamation is approved under section 245 of the RMA,—

  - (2) All jurisdictions, offices, appointments, Orders in Council, orders, warrants, rules, regulations, seals, forms, books, records, and instruments that relate to the Environment Court and originated under the RMA have full effect as if they had originated under the corresponding provisions of this Act and, as necessary, must be treated as having originated under this Act. Jurisdiction of Planning Tribunal in relation to certain decisions under RMA
## Clause 68 — Planning Tribunal has jurisdiction to review specified RMA decisions
  - (1) Part 4 of Schedule 12 comes into force on a date specified by Order in Council made under section 2(5) (the appointed date).
  - (2) On and from the appointed date,—
  - (a) a right of objection to the consent authority relating to a specified decision under the RMA is replaced with a right of review to the Planning Tribunal (see Part 4 of Schedule 12); and
  - (b) a review of a specified decision must be carried out in accordance with Schedule 10 of this Act instead of the RMA; and
  - (c) any appeal or review of a decision of the Planning Tribunal in reviewing a specified decision must be made under Schedule 10.
  - (3) The right of review may be exercised only in respect of specified decisions made on or after the appointed date.
  - (4) The Planning Tribunal has jurisdiction to review a specified decision in accordance with Schedule 10, which applies as if a reference to a consent or permit were a reference to a resource consent.
  - (5) Despite subclause
  - (2), the following must continue to be dealt with by a consent authority:
  - (a) an objection lodged with the consent authority that is pending immediately before the appointed date:
  - (b) an objection lodged on or after the appointed date that relates to an event that occurred before that date.
  - (6) In this clause, specified decision means a decision under the RMA that the tribunal is empowered to review on and from the appointed date.

## Clause 69 — Regulations may make consequential amendments to other secondary
  - legislation
  - (1) The Governor-General may, by Order in Council made on recommendation of the Minister, make regulations that amend any secondary legislation to make amendments that are consequential on the passing of this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (2) The same set of regulations made under this clause may amend 1 or more instruments that are secondary legislation.
  - (3) Regulations made under this clause may make transitional and savings provisions as necessary to reflect in the secondary legislation the transition from the RMA to this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (4) This clause does not limit section 41 of the Legislation Act 2019.
  - (5) Regulations made under this section are secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
  - Compare: 2023 No 46 Schedule 1 cl 86
## Clause 70 — Amendment of RMA national direction instruments to remove redundant
  - or duplicative provisions
  - (1) During the transition period,—
  - (a) the Minister for the Environment may amend a national environmental standard, a national policy statement, or a national planning instrument as if section 44(3), 53(2)(a), or 58H(2) of the RMA applies:
  - (b) the Minister of Conservation may amend the New Zealand coastal policy statement or provisions of the national planning standards that relate to the coastal marine area as if section 53(2)(a) or 58H(2) of the RMA applies.
  - (2) The Minister must be satisfied that—
  - (a) the content would be more efficiently addressed through the processes in this Act or the Natural Environment Act 2026; or
  - (b) the content is redundant because of the transition from the RMA to this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - Compare: 2023 No 46 Schedule 1 cl 14
## Clause 71 — Heritage protection authority approvals and orders
  - (1) All approvals under section 188 of the RMA of heritage protection authorities in effect immediately before the specified transition date cease to apply.
  - (2) A territorial authority must have regard to whether and how any heritage order in effect in its district or region immediately before the specified transition date should be reflected in rules or policies, when preparing its first land use plan.
## Clause 72 — Reference to national environmental standard includes initial national
  - standard A reference in other legislation other than the RMA to a national environmental standard under the RMA must be treated as including a reference to any applicable initial national standard.
## Clause 73 — Application of clause 4.1(2) of National Policy Statement for Highly
  - Productive Land 2022 disapplied The suspension of the requirement for regional councils to map areas of highly productive land in their regions until 31 December 2027 in clause 4.1(2) of the National Policy Statement for Highly Productive Land 2022 is further suspended until the specified transition date.
## Clause 74 — Relief where land previously subject to similar RMA rule becomes subject
  - to specified rule
  - (1) This clause applies if—
  - (a) the first proposed plan includes a specified rule that applies to land; and
  - (b) that land was subject to an RMA rule that had a similar impact to the specified rule.
  - (2) If this clause applies, when the first proposed plan is made operative, a person who owns land to which the specified rule applies is eligible for relief under the relief framework in the plan if—
  - (a) the specified rule has a significant adverse impact on the reasonable use of that land; and
  - (b) the land has not changed ownership between the time that the RMA rule was first notified and the time that the first proposed plan is notified.
  - (3) In this clause,— first proposed plan means the first proposed land use plan or proposed natural environment plan RMA rule means a rule in an operative district plan or regional plan under the RMA for the district or region in which the land is located.
## Clause 75 — Agreement under RMA defining landward boundary of coastal marine
  - area An agreement defined in section 2(1) of the RMA, or a declaration under section 310(e) of the RMA, that determines the mouth of a river for the purposes of defining the landward boundary of the coastal marine area—
  - (a) continues to have effect for the purposes of defining the landward boundary of the coastal marine area under this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026; and
  - (b) ceases to have effect on the date that the mouth of the river is determined in accordance with section 352 of the Natural Environment Act 2026.
## Clause 76 — Emergency works authorised by RMA
  - (1) Sections 330A, 330B, and 331 of the RMA continue to apply in relation to any activity to which those provisions apply that are undertaken by or on behalf of a person, authority, network utility operator, or lifeline utility before the specified transition date.
  - (2) A resource consent that is required to be sought under section 330A(2) or 330B(3) of the RMA must be processed and determined under the RMA on the basis provided in clause 19, even if the application is lodged after the specified transition date.
## Clause 77 — Preparation of first natural environment plan to consider PC1
  - (1) In the preparation of the first natural environment plan applying to the Waikato and Waipa River catchments under the Natural Environment Act 2026, the responsible local authority must have particular regard to PC1.
  - (2) Subclause
  - (1) applies in addition to the obligations in sections 9, 10, and 11 of both this Act and the Natural Environment Act 2026 and does not limit, qualify, or otherwise affect those obligations.
  - (3) Subclause
  - (1) does not apply to subsequent reviews, amendments, plan changes, or replacement plans under the Natural Environment Act 2026.
  - (4) In this clause, PC1 has the meaning given to it in clause 71 of Schedule 12 of the RMA.
## Clause 78 — Provisions relating to Waikato Regional Council’s PC1
  - (1) On the repeal of the RMA, clause 71 of Schedule 12 of the RMA continues in effect on its terms.
  - (2) This clause is repealed on the close of 31 December 2032.
